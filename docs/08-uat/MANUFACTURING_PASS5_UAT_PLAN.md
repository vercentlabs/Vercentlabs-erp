# Manufacturing Pass 5 UAT Plan

UAT personas: manufacturing engineer, planner, supervisor, operator/material controller, quality inspector, subcontract buyer, maintenance planner and cost accountant. Evidence must cover engineering change/effectivity, multi-level explosion, MRP shortage/pegging, capacity conflict, work release, shop-floor actuals, partial/backflush material flow, batch/serial output, quality hold/rework, subcontracting, maintenance downtime, completion costing/variance and production-to-Stock/Accounting reconciliation on desktop plus tablet/shop-floor form factors.
