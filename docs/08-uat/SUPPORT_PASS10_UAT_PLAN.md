# Support Pass 10 UAT Plan

1. Create the same inbound email twice; verify one ticket/comment and one routing/notification effect.
2. Customer replies by email while supervisor reassigns; verify no message loss and deterministic owner/history.
3. Agent adds private note + attachment; customer portal, public reply email, search and export must not reveal it.
4. Exercise business-hours SLA with pending-customer pause, resume, DST/holiday boundary, risk alert and one escalation per level.
5. Simultaneous routing/reassignment conflict returns a recoverable stale-state result; history shows the winner/override reason.
6. Merge duplicate tickets; verify canonical redirect and all source conversation/attachments/history remain reconstructable. Reopen the canonical ticket under policy.
7. Portal customer tries guessed ticket/attachment IDs from another customer; verify no data/metadata leakage.
8. Link order/product/asset and evaluate warranty/service entitlement with effective-date source evidence and governed override.
9. Convert a product-quality complaint to Quality investigation; retry request without duplicate NCR/investigation.
10. Resolve ticket, collect CSAT once, verify agent/SLA dashboard formulas and complete audit trail across desktop/tablet/phone/keyboard/screen reader.
