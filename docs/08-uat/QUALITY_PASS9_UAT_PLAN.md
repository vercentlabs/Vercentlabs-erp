# Quality Pass 9 UAT Plan

1. Configure/approve effective standard, specification, plan, control point, sampling and tolerance; prove historical inspection keeps exact version after supersession.
2. Receive supplier lot, perform incoming sample/measurement/pass-fail, fail it, verify automatic hold and NCR.
3. Attempt issue/transfer/pick/ship concurrently with active hold; every applicable movement must be blocked. Release only part/authorized scope and prove only released scope can move.
4. In-process inspection fails; execute approved rework/scrap and reinspection without duplicate effects on retry.
5. Execute return-to-supplier and verify Procurement/Stock/Accounting public references and reconciliation.
6. Use-as-is requires authorized maker-checker/reason/evidence and cannot be self-approved under configured policy.
7. Create CAPA from NCR, implement actions, fail first effectiveness check, reopen, then close after independent evidence.
8. Try measurement with expired/out-of-tolerance instrument; verify block/impact-assessment path and calibration lineage.
9. Trace complaint/finished lot back through receipt/production/inspection/hold/disposition and issue approved CoA from exact result/spec versions.
10. Verify quality-cost/KPI formulas, permissions/audit, responsive tablet/desktop behavior and WCAG keyboard/screen-reader states.
