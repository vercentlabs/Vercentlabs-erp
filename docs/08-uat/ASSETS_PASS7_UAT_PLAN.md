# Assets Pass 7 UAT Plan

Representative sign-off journeys:
1. Capital PO/receipt/invoice → one asset → maker-checker capitalization → Accounting reconciliation.
2. Assign → handover → transfer location/department/custodian → full movement history.
3. Straight-line/declining/units-of-production schedule → approval/posting → closed-period rejection → reversal/reconciliation.
4. Revaluation/impairment effective date and future schedule behavior.
5. Preventive maintenance due → technician execution → Stock parts → downtime → Manufacturing availability recovery.
6. Warranty repair and repair-history evidence.
7. Inspection/calibration due → mobile evidence/certificate → fail/corrective action/overdue policy.
8. Physical verification campaign with matched/missing/moved/unexpected discrepancies and scan fallback.
9. Asset sale and scrap → gain/loss → Accounting result → reversal test.
10. Unauthorized technician/custodian attempts financial/disposal actions; cross-company search/aggregate leakage test.

Each case records visible outcome, database/audit evidence, downstream references, exception/reversal behavior and role sign-off.
