## Module Profiles

### CRM
**Purpose:** revenue-team system of record — captures leads from any channel, qualifies/routes them, manages account/contact/opportunity lifecycle to a won deal, with privacy and offline-mobile governance built in. **Personas:** sales reps/managers, marketing ops, CS/ops, channel/partner managers, RevOps, compliance/privacy officers. **Problems solved:** fragmented lead intake, leads going stale with no SLA discipline, undisciplined pipeline stage-skipping, no unified customer 360, non-auditable data-subject requests, reps disconnected from data in the field.

Capability groups: **Core engine** (generic CRUD; playbook-gated stage exit; idempotent lead→customer conversion creating account+contact+opportunity — `services/api/src/crm.js:2110,2330-2453`); **Lead acquisition & intelligence** (CSV import with preview-commit-rollback, signed webhooks, chat widget, scoring, business-hours SLA, nurture ranking, assignment policies — `crm/lead-acquisition.js`, `lead-intelligence.js`, `lead-operations.js`); **Communications & conversation intelligence** (OAuth email/calendar sync, meeting booking, signed telephony webhooks, click-to-call, transcription — `communications.js`, `conversation-intelligence.js`); **Marketing execution** (SQL-compiled segments, branching journeys, A/B variants, frequency caps, surveys — `marketing-execution.js`); **Customer success & account intelligence** (health scoring, churn workflow, Customer 360, GDPR-style privacy request preview→execute — `customer-success.js`, `account-intelligence.js`); **Partner engagement & AI** (deal registration/conflict detection, MDF, next-best-action, PII-redacted AI drafts — `partner-engagement.js`, `ai-intelligence.js`).

Cross-module: opportunity → Sales quotation (`source_opportunity_id`); Sales order confirmation writes back `crm_opportunities.status='won'` (`sales/index.js:1867-1873`). Automations: event-driven rule engine with per-rule savepoint isolation; playbook-gated stage transitions. Reports: 14 types incl. pipeline, conversion, forecast, revenue-operations, account-health, AI-governance. Mobile: leads/opportunities/activities/pipeline are **native + offline** with sync/conflict resolution; deeper sub-modules are secure-browser-handoff. Security: route+service-layer permission checks, CSRF/origin guard, optimistic-concurrency stage-move guards, AI PII redaction.

**Best marketing angle:** public capture form → scored/SLA-tracked lead → one-click account+opportunity conversion → pipeline stage-exit blocked until qualification questions are answered — synced offline on mobile with conflict-safe queuing.

### Sales
**Purpose:** quote-to-cash engine turning a priced, tax-compliant, versioned quotation into a credit-checked order with auditable handoff to Accounting. **Personas:** sales reps/managers, sales ops/finance approvers, billing/finance teams, buyer's own procurement contact. **Problems solved:** manual/error-prone GST-aware pricing, untracked quote revisions, orders exceeding customer credit, no clean order-to-fulfillment/invoice linkage, no self-service quote acceptance.

Capability groups: **Quotation pricing/tax engine** (price-list→pricing-rule→override waterfall; automatic CGST/SGST vs IGST split by state; margin redaction — `sales/index.js:285`); **Quotation governance** (threshold-based auto-approval vs. approval-request, version diffing — `quotation-governance.js`); **Order lifecycle & credit control** (idempotent quote→order promotion; advisory-locked customer credit-exposure check at confirmation — `sales/index.js:1756`; holds, controlled amendment, return requests — `order-governance.js`); **Fulfillment/invoice handoff** (idempotency-keyed request objects); **Public quote acceptance** (SHA-256-hashed share tokens, view tracking, typed-signature accept/reject — `apps/web/src/app/quote/[token]/page.tsx`).

Cross-module: CRM opportunity → quotation (`source_opportunity_id`); quotation → order (`source_quotation_id`); order confirm → CRM `status='won'`; order → Accounting via `createInvoiceFromSalesRequest` (`accounting/receivables.js:236`). Automations: threshold-driven auto-approval branching; auto-cancel of pending approvals on quote revision; every action writes a `sales_document_events` row. Reports: quotation-conversion, order-intake, expiring-quotations, pending-approvals, fulfillment, billing-readiness, margin. Mobile: secure-browser-handoff only. Security: 15+ granular `sales.*` permissions enforced at the service layer; uniform margin redaction; immutable event trail.

**Best marketing angle:** multi-currency GST-aware quote → public accept-with-signature → one-click order conversion → real-time credit check before shipment → idempotent, auditable Accounting handoff.

### Accounting
**Purpose:** multi-company, multi-currency GL and financial-operations system — receivables, payables, banking, fixed assets, tax, period close with approval governance and native India GST compliance. **Personas:** controllers/CFOs, accountants/bookkeepers, AP/AR clerks, tax/compliance officers, treasury/cash managers, auditors. **Problems solved:** spreadsheet-driven close, duplicate/erroneous payments, manual AR/AP aging, bolt-on GST compliance, unauthorized postings.

Capability groups: **GL & Journals** (draft→approve→post lifecycle; auto-computed year-end closing journal — `close.js:413-468`); **Receivables** (invoices auto-generated from Sales — `receivables.js:239-323`; aging, collections/dunning); **Payables** (vendor bills importable from matched procurement records; 2/3-way matching with tolerance and mandatory-reason override — `matching.js`; duplicate-invoice-number blocking); **Banking & Reconciliation** (CSV statement import with SHA-256 integrity hash); **Fixed Assets subledger** (GL-account-mapped categories; straight-line/declining-balance/units-of-production depreciation — `accounting/assets.js`); **Tax & Compliance** (CGST/SGST/IGST/TDS/TCS ledger with HSN/SAC and reverse-charge; GST e-invoice/e-way bill idempotent compliance requests — `compliance.js`); **Close/Planning/Consolidation** (7-task governed close checklist with blocking dependencies; multi-entity consolidation; intercompany posting; cash-forecast scenarios; budget lifecycle).

Cross-module: Payables↔Procurement (confirmed); Receivables↔Sales (confirmed, `sales_invoice_requests`). **Note:** the Fixed-Asset subledger here is a separate data model from the EAM Assets module — no shared table/FK (see Honest Limitations). Automations: subledger approval engine with self-approval block and content-hash tamper detection; scheduled recurring journals, accruals, FX revaluation, consolidation, dunning; every transition logged to `accounting_events`. Reports: 16-report registry incl. trial-balance, GL, P&L, balance-sheet, cash-flow, aged AR/AP, tax-summary, close-status, subledger-reconciliation, plus a hashed close-pack snapshot bundle. Mobile: secure-browser-handoff only. Security: 26-entry permission set incl. dedicated audit view; company-scoping on nearly every query; maker-checker approval; period lock/close controls; immutable event trail.

**Best marketing angle:** real 3-way PO/receipt/invoice matching that gates AP posting, paired with a governed close that mechanically blocks completion until every subledger/bank/tax exception is resolved.

### Procurement
**Purpose:** source-to-pay control tower governing requisition→sourcing→ordering→receiving→invoice-matching with policy-driven readiness scoring at every stage. **Personas:** procurement/purchasing managers, category/sourcing buyers, supplier-qualification officers, receiving clerks, AP/finance staff, requesting employees. **Problems solved:** maverick/off-contract spend, supplier risk blind spots, invoice-to-PO fraud/overbilling, approval bottlenecks, lack of procurement audit trail.

Capability groups: **Requisition-to-order lifecycle** (draft→submit→approve→execute state machines with PO amendment workflow); **Sourcing & supplier management** (supplier sites/qualifications/certifications/scorecards; RFQ invitations/bids/evaluations/awards); **Receiving & matching** (2/3/4-way match with per-line variance tolerance and mandatory-reason override — `governance.js`); **Governance/control tower** (policy-configurable readiness rules, exception-case queue, content-hashed audit snapshots); **Analytics** (12 pre-built reports: spend-analysis, maverick-spend, matching-exceptions, supplier-risk, cycle-time).

Cross-module: confirmed enforced chain requisition→RFQ→award→PO→receipt; AP import gated on `procurement_matching_records.status='matched'` (`accounting/payables.js:250-280`); Stock only loosely linked (reorder-rule supplier reference, no auto-requisition job found). Automations: self-approval blocked; auto exception-case creation on match failure. Reports: full dashboard plus 12-report registry. Mobile: generic fallback resource list only — **no entry in primary mobile navigation**. Security: fine-grained per-resource/action permissions; self-approval prevention; append-only audit trail with content-hashed governance snapshots.

**Best marketing angle:** an invoice-matching engine that mechanically blocks AP bill creation on unresolved variance, paired with live readiness scoring across the whole requisition-to-payment chain.

### Stock / Warehouse
**Purpose:** real-time, multi-location inventory ledger tracking on-hand quantity, reservation, and valuation per item/warehouse/bin/batch. **Personas:** warehouse/inventory staff, inventory planners, operations managers, transfer-execution staff. **Problems solved:** stockouts/overstock, inventory valuation accuracy, multi-warehouse/bin visibility, batch/serial traceability, unbalanced adjustments.

Capability groups: **Ledger & balances** (row-locked movement posting enforcing available-stock checks — `stock/index.js:86-174`); **Movement types** (receipt/issue/transfer/adjustment/return/count, permission-gated); **Transfers** (atomic issue+receipt pair posting); **Costing & valuation** (configurable moving-average/FIFO costing); **Traceability & planning** (batch/serial tracking, reservations, reorder rules feeding a low-stock dashboard); **Warehouse structure** (typed warehouses, hierarchical bins).

Cross-module: **confirmed wired** to Manufacturing and POS (both post real stock movements); **not wired** to standard Sales-order fulfillment (order lines carry `warehouseId` but no stock-movement call found — see Honest Limitations). Automations: transfer completion auto-posts matching issue/receipt pair. Reports: single dashboard (qty, value, reserved, low-stock) — no dedicated reports registry. Mobile: **zero entries** in navigation — no mobile access path. Security: permission-gated, row-level locking, idempotency keys; **no approval workflow** (thinner governance than Procurement).

**Best marketing angle:** one shared, race-safe inventory ledger that Manufacturing and POS both post into live — stated precisely, without implying Sales-order fulfillment already deducts stock.

### Manufacturing
**Purpose:** plan BOMs and work orders, execute shop-floor production, post material consumption/finished-goods output directly into the live Stock ledger. **Personas:** production planners, shop-floor supervisors, work-center/plant managers, finance/costing roles. **Problems solved:** stale/unapproved product structures, releasing work that can't be built, manual inventory adjustments for production, blind material shortages.

Capability groups: **BOM management** (versioned, multi-component, scrap %, manual/backflush issue method, draft→active lifecycle, one active BOM per item enforced); **Routings & work centers** (ordered operations with timing/capacity/rate); **Work order lifecycle** (draft→planned→released→in_progress→completed, materials/operations snapshotted from active BOM/routing); **Production posting** (per-unit material issue + finished-goods receipt posted as real stock movements with shortage/over-production guards — `manufacturing/index.js:417-613`); **Material/capacity planning** (MRP-style runs producing recommended purchase/manufacture/transfer/expedite actions); **Costing** (cost snapshots at planned/actual/completion checkpoints).

Cross-module: confirmed — BOM components reference stock items; work order creation copies BOM; `postProduction` writes real, auditable stock movements with FK to the posting. Automations: event-sourced audit trail; policy toggles (`allow_overproduction`, `backflush_materials`); no scheduler found — planning runs are manual. Reports: single dashboard (active/planned/completed WOs, live shortage count). Mobile: **absent entirely** — web-only. Security: fine-grained permissions with owner bypass, forced RLS, state-machine guards.

**Best marketing angle:** a released work order cannot proceed without proven component availability, and every material issue/finished-goods receipt is a real, auditable stock movement — not a paper work-order tracker sitting beside a separately managed inventory.

### Quality
**Purpose:** enforce inspection checkpoints and disposition control across incoming receipts, in-process manufacturing, stock, and returns, with governed release and CAPA-driven resolution. **Personas:** QA inspectors, QA managers/release authorities, plant quality engineers, supplier quality managers, compliance/audit leads. **Problems solved:** bad material/product silently entering stock or shipping, lack of segregation of duties between inspection and release, one-off defect notes instead of tracked corrective action, no supplier performance visibility.

Capability groups: **Quality plans & inspection points** (reusable plans typed by stage: incoming/in_process/final/stock_audit/supplier/customer_return); **Inspection execution & release** (release is a separate, governed step from completion); **Holds & disposition** (automatic inventory hold on failed inspection); **Non-conformance & CAPA** (severity-graded NC → linked CAPA with root-cause, corrective/preventive action, effectiveness verification); **Supplier quality & audits** (periodic scorecards, structured audits with findings linkable to CAPA); **Traceability** (append-only `quality_events` log).

Cross-module: `source_type` schema spans procurement receipts, stock batches/serials, manufacturing work orders/postings, sales/POS returns — a genuine cross-module inspection framework. Automations: auto-hold on inspection failure (togglable); hard-coded self-release block. Reports: single dashboard (open/failed/today's inspections, active holds, open NC/CAPA). Mobile: absent — web-only. Security: **the inspector cannot release their own inspection** (hard-coded segregation of duties); forced RLS.

**Best marketing angle:** "no one signs off their own work" — self-release block plus automatic inventory holds on failed inspections, spanning the entire physical-goods lifecycle from receiving dock to customer return.

### Assets (Enterprise Asset Management)
**Purpose:** track and govern the full lifecycle of physical/equipment assets — acquisition, capitalization, assignment, maintenance, disposal — with an audit trail for every state change. **Personas:** facilities/IT/operations managers, maintenance technicians, finance/asset controllers, auditors. **Problems solved:** unclear custody of equipment, unproven preventive maintenance, unauditable capitalization/disposal, unmonitored warranty/maintenance-due dates.

Capability groups: **Asset register & categorization**; **Capitalization** (draft→available; creator cannot capitalize their own asset — self-approval blocked); **Assignment & custody** (enforced-unique active assignment); **Maintenance** (planned/preventive/priority orders capturing downtime hours, labor/parts/external cost — parts consumption is a real stock movement); **Disposal** (computed gain/loss, also self-approval blocked); **Inspections & transfers** (data model present, but no create-function found — read-only today, see Honest Limitations).

Cross-module: links to Procurement (PO/receipt/vendor-bill IDs), Stock (maintenance-parts stock movement), Support (`related_asset_id`); identity fields align with but aren't FK-joined to HR. Automations: dashboard due-window logic; status-transition guards; self-approval blocking. Reports: single dashboard (total, assigned, in-maintenance, warranties expiring, net book value, maintenance due). Mobile: no entry — no mobile presence. Security: full permission surface; self-approval blocking; immutable event log; forced RLS.

**Best marketing angle:** acquisition → capitalization (self-approval blocked) → named custodian assignment → maintenance work orders with real labor/parts/external cost capture → disposal with computed gain/loss (also self-approval-blocked).

### Projects
**Purpose:** plan, staff, execute, and financially track billable/internal projects, connecting tasks and timesheets to real project cost and margin. **Personas:** project/delivery managers, consultants/employees logging time, finance/PMO staff, sales/account managers. **Problems solved:** not knowing project profitability until after close, double-entering billing into Sales/Accounting, budget overruns discovered too late, unclear approval accountability on timesheets.

Capability groups: **Project setup & staffing** (billing method: fixed/T&M/milestone/non-billable; customer/sales-order linkage); **Work breakdown** (tasks, milestones, subtasking); **Time & expense** (time entries with cost/bill rate; owner cannot self-approve their own entry); **Budgeting** (versioned budgets split into labor/expense/procurement/contingency/revenue); **Lifecycle governance** (cannot complete a project with open tasks); **Profitability & billing** (live gross-margin calc from approved labor+expense+procurement actuals vs. billed revenue — `projects/index.js:363-409`; idempotency-keyed billing milestones).

Cross-module: `project_procurement_links` feed real procurement actuals into profitability; `sales_order_id`/invoice IDs link outbound to Sales/Accounting; `related_project_id` on Support tickets. Automations: hard rule blocking completion with open tasks; self-approval block on time entries. Reports: dashboard plus a per-project profitability report. Mobile: **no mobile presence** — a real gap for field time entry. Security: broad permission surface; self-approval block; RLS-enforced audit log.

**Best marketing angle:** live gross-margin computed from approved labor/expense/procurement actuals against contracted revenue while the project is still open, plus an idempotent billing-milestone handoff into Sales/Accounting — a running-code profitability engine, not a static report.

### Point of Sale
**Purpose:** governed retail checkout — cashiers ring up sales, take mixed-tender payment, reconcile cash — with every sale deducting real inventory in real time. **Personas:** store cashiers/associates, shift supervisors, store/finance admins. **Problems solved:** disconnect between floor sales and back-office stock/cash, unauthorized over/under-charging, indefensible end-of-day cash reconciliation.

Capability groups: **Checkout & cart completion** (multi-line sale, mixed payments, idempotency-key protected — `point-of-sale/index.js:241-448`); **Shift & cash-drawer control** (opening float, close-with-variance calc; one open shift per terminal enforced by a unique index); **Returns & refunds** (line-level, policy-gated approval); **Store/terminal/pricing setup**; **Reconciliation** (per-payment-method expected/counted/variance tracking).

Cross-module: confirmed — every sale line posts a stock deduction in the same transaction; **not wired** — `pos_sales.sales_order_id`/`accounting_invoice_id` columns exist but are never populated (see Honest Limitations). Automations: server-computed cash-variance at shift close; policy-driven return approval. Reports: single dashboard. Mobile: **explicitly disabled** in the module catalog — web/desktop-only. Security: permission-gated, forced RLS, price-override permission gate.

**Best marketing angle:** every checkout deducts real inventory in the same database transaction as the sale — no nightly sync, no phantom stock.

### Support / Customer Service
**Purpose:** ticket-based system tracking requests from creation through SLA-timed resolution, with full context links to the customer/account and the commercial record that triggered the issue. **Personas:** support agents, team leads/queue managers, resolution owners, service ops/CS managers. **Problems solved:** silently missed SLA deadlines, agents lacking commercial context, tickets closed without documented resolution.

Cross-module: confirmed, caller-supplied — tickets carry `customer_id`/`contact_id` (CRM), `related_sales_order_id`/`related_invoice_id` (Sales/Accounting), `related_asset_id` (Assets), `related_project_id` (Projects), `related_quality_record_id` (Quality); dedicated customer-history view. **Best marketing angle:** every ticket automatically carries the customer's commercial context (CRM account, sales order, invoice, asset, project) via first-class relationship columns and a dedicated customer-history view.

### HR & Payroll
**Purpose:** tenant/company-scoped system of record for employee lifecycle, attendance/leave, and payroll processing, enforced through the same permission/audit/separation-of-duties patterns as the rest of the platform. **Personas:** HR managers/admins, payroll preparers, line managers, finance/controllers, employees. **Problems solved:** employee master data in spreadsheets, manual leave-balance tracking, error-prone attendance-to-pay math, no maker-checker on payroll/leave approval.

Capability groups: **Workforce structure** (departments, designations, shifts, manager hierarchy, probation/confirmation dates); **Attendance & leave** (per-day status/overtime; leave balances with automatic deduction on approval); **Compensation & statutory setup** (salary components, effective-dated history, jurisdiction-aware statutory components — PF/ESI/professional tax/TDS/gratuity/bonus); **Payroll run processing** (draft→calculated→pending_approval→approved→posted; attendance-adjusted gross-pay calculation in one transaction; **self-approval blocked**); **Employee expenses** (table exists, exposed via generic resource API).

### Shared Platform
**Purpose:** the cross-cutting control plane every module runs on — multi-tenant/multi-company isolation, RBAC, governed approvals, immutable audit, workflow/reporting primitives, a parallel mobile API surface, subscription billing. **Personas:** organisation owners/sysadmins, company admins, per-module managers, auditors, IT/compliance admins. **Problems solved:** cross-tenant data leakage risk, ad hoc access-flag security models, unenforceable approval paths, unreliable audit trails, ungated tenant access on non-payment.

Capability groups: **Tenant & company structure** (organizations/companies/branches/departments/cost-centers/teams with granular membership-scoped access — company/branch/department, not all-or-nothing); **Roles & permissions** (12 seeded system roles, time-bound role assignments with start/expiry dates, framework-neutral permission-key packages per module); **Approvals & workflow engine** (generic approval-requests table plus a governed command registry with reusable decision/separation-of-duties primitives reused across Accounting, Sales, CRM, HR, Assets, Projects, POS, Support); **Audit trail** (a database trigger makes the audit table immutable — UPDATE/DELETE are rejected at the Postgres level, not just the app level); **Reporting/document/localization primitives** (injection-safe CSV export with hard-capped pagination; MIME/size-allow-listed, SHA-256-hashed, quarantine-lifecycle document storage; India-default localization — en-IN/Asia-Kolkata/INR, April-start fiscal year).

Cross-module: underlies every module — org/company/branch scoping, shared roles/permissions, shared numbering series, module-catalog-driven entitlement gating. Automations: one-transaction organisation bootstrap (seeds 12 roles, ~29 numbering series); idempotent Razorpay webhook pipeline (HMAC-verified, replay-protected, advisory-lock-serialized). Mobile: ~90 web routes mapped to native or secure-browser-handoff delivery; device-bound auth (fingerprint-required login, secure-store tokens, biometric re-lock after 15s background). **HR & Payroll and Stock are the notable mobile-access gaps.** Security: login hardening (failed-attempt lockout, password history); **MFA columns exist but are explicitly not yet enforced** (see Honest Limitations); session governance; structural (not just app-layer) data isolation; non-bypassable audit trail.

**Best marketing angle:** "one control plane, twelve modules" — multi-tenant isolation, a real time-bound/risk-tiered role model, a database-level-immutable audit log, and governed maker-checker approvals are shared infrastructure every module inherits, not bolted on per-module.

## Cross-Module Workflows

1. **Quote-to-Cash** (CRM → Sales → Accounting): lead capture → scoring/SLA → lead-to-account+contact+opportunity conversion → playbook-gated pipeline → quotation seeded from opportunity → threshold-based approval → public token quote acceptance with typed signature → order conversion → advisory-locked credit check at confirmation (writes CRM `status='won'`) → invoice request → posted customer invoice. Evidence: `crm.js:2110`, `sales/index.js:645-1873`, `accounting/receivables.js:236`.
2. **Procure-to-Pay** (Procurement → Accounting): requisition → sourcing/RFQ → award → purchase order → receipt → 2/3/4-way match → vendor bill import gated on `matched` status → payment. Evidence: `procurement/index.js:548-578,1874-2255`, `accounting/payables.js:250-280`.
3. **Make-to-Stock Production** (Manufacturing → Stock): active BOM → work order (materials snapshotted) → release blocked on shortage → start → production posting issues raw material and receives finished goods as real stock movements in one transaction. Evidence: `manufacturing/index.js:267-613`.
4. **Retail Checkout-to-Inventory** (POS → Stock): cart build → availability pre-check → checkout posts sale and stock deduction in the same transaction → shift-close cash-variance reconciliation. Evidence: `point-of-sale/index.js:198-404`.
5. **Physical-Goods Quality Gate** (Quality → Procurement/Manufacturing/Stock/Sales/POS): quality plan typed by stage → inspection tied to a source (receipt, work order, batch/serial, return) → failed inspection auto-creates an inventory hold → release requires a different person than the inspector. Evidence: `quality/index.js:299-393`.
6. **Enterprise Asset Acquisition-to-Disposal** (Assets → Procurement → Stock → Support): asset referencing PO/receipt/vendor-bill → capitalization (self-approval blocked) → custodian assignment → maintenance consuming spare parts via a real stock movement → disposal computing gain/loss (also self-approval-blocked) → Support tickets can reference the asset. Evidence: `assets/index.js:112-457`. *Does not connect to Accounting's separate fixed-asset subledger — see Honest Limitations.*
7. **Billable Project Delivery** (Projects → Procurement → Sales → Accounting): project linked to a sales order → tasks/timesheets (self-approval blocked) → real procurement actuals feed profitability → live gross-margin calc → idempotent billing milestone → invoice request → posted customer invoice. Evidence: `projects/index.js:131-409`.
8. **Support Ticket Contextual Resolution** (Support → CRM/Sales/Accounting/Assets/Projects/Quality): ticket carries first-class references to account, sales order, invoice, asset, project, quality record → SLA-driven due date → queue-strategy assignment → resolution requires a code → aggregated customer-history view. Evidence: `support/index.js:158-221`.
9. **Governed Payroll-to-Books** (HR & Payroll → Accounting): attendance/leave → attendance-adjusted payroll calculation → approval (self-approval blocked) → posted, capturing a manual accounting-batch reference. Evidence: `hr-payroll/index.js:271-534`. *Reference field only, not an automated journal-posting pipeline — see Honest Limitations.*
10. **Platform-Wide Governed Approval Backbone** (Shared Platform → Accounting/Sales/CRM and, by pattern, HR/Assets/Projects/POS/Support): a single validated, permission-gated command registry wires module approvals onto shared decision and separation-of-duties primitives. Evidence: `packages/workflows/src/index.js`, `apps/web/src/lib/approval-commands.ts`.
11. **Tenant Onboarding & Module Entitlement** (Shared Platform → all 12 modules): signup → organisation bootstrap seeds 12 roles and ~29 numbering series → subscription activation (HMAC-verified, replay-protected webhook) → per-module entitlement gate respected by every module's UI/API. Evidence: `apps/web/src/lib/platform.ts:610-817`.
12. **Mobile Field-Rep Offline Pipeline** (Shared Platform mobile auth → CRM): device-fingerprinted login → secure-store tokens → CRM leads/opportunities/activities/pipeline sync natively offline with a queued mutation pipeline and server-side optimistic-concurrency conflict resolution. Evidence: `apps/mobile/src/modules/crm/manifest.ts`, `data/sync.ts`.
13. **Returns & Reverse Logistics** (Sales/POS → Stock → Quality → Accounting): a sales or POS return creates a restocking stock movement, can be routed through a return-typed Quality inspection for disposition, and flows back into Accounting via the same receivables/payables objects as the original transaction. Evidence: `sales/order-governance.js:1052`, migration `049_quality_module.sql`.

## Publicly Usable Product Evidence

Concrete, honest, code-backed claims safe to use in later phases' copy:

- Real 2/3/4-way matching between purchase order, goods receipt, and vendor invoice gates AP bill creation.
- Period close is a governed, task-gated workflow (subledgers → bank → tax → accruals → FX → review → lock) that mechanically blocks completion until every exception is resolved.
- Customers can accept or reject a sales quotation via a public, single-use, hashed share link with a typed digital signature — no login required.
- Sales orders are checked against real-time aggregated customer credit exposure, under an advisory database lock, before confirmation.
- GST tax calculation automatically splits CGST/SGST vs. IGST by state; e-invoice and e-way bill compliance requests are idempotent with retry handling.
- A released manufacturing work order cannot proceed without proven component availability; every material issue/finished-goods receipt posts a real, auditable stock movement.
- A point-of-sale checkout deducts live inventory in the same database transaction as the sale — no nightly batch sync.
- Quality inspections require a different person to release a lot than the person who inspected it — non-bypassable segregation of duties.
- Failed quality inspections automatically place an inventory hold on the affected batch, serial, receipt, work order, or return.
- Enterprise asset capitalization and disposal both block the asset's own creator from approving their own transaction.
- Payroll runs require a different approver than the preparer; leave requests cannot be approved by the person who submitted them.
- Every audit log entry is immutable at the database level — a Postgres trigger rejects UPDATE/DELETE even against application bugs.
- Role assignments can be time-bound and scoped to specific companies, branches, or departments rather than all-or-nothing.
- Billing webhooks are HMAC-signature-verified, replay-protected, and processed under an advisory lock per organization.
- CRM's lead-to-opportunity conversion and pipeline stage-exit gating are real, idempotent backend operations, not UI-only workflows.

## Honest Limitations (do not overclaim these)

- **Standard sales-order fulfillment does not yet post an automatic stock deduction** — only Manufacturing and POS write to the stock ledger today. Do not imply every sales order automatically reduces inventory.
- **POS sales do not yet automatically create a Sales order or an Accounting invoice/journal** — the linking columns exist but are unpopulated. Do not claim POS-to-books is fully automated.
- **The Accounting fixed-asset subledger and the EAM Assets module are independent data models with no code-level link**, despite a UI-labeled "accounting handoff" permission. Do not claim a single unified asset-to-depreciation pipeline.
- **HR payroll's link to Accounting is a stored reference ID, not an automated journal-posting integration.** Do not claim automatic payroll-to-GL posting.
- **No native mobile presence** exists for Manufacturing, Quality, Assets, Projects, Point of Sale, Support, Stock, or HR & Payroll; POS and Support are explicitly disabled in the mobile module catalog. Mobile claims should be scoped to CRM (native, offline) plus selected Procurement/platform workspaces (secure-browser-handoff), never "full ERP on mobile."
- **MFA columns exist in the schema but are explicitly not yet enforced** ("MFA-ready foundation," per the platform's own in-app language) — do not claim MFA is active.
- Several data models exist without a working write path yet: Support escalation automation (schema present, no code path creates escalation rows), Assets inspections/transfers/maintenance-plans (read-only resources, no create-function found), Projects task dependencies (data model present, no dependency-management function found), Projects expenses (read-only in the sampled code).
- Procurement has **no entry in primary mobile navigation** (only a generic fallback resource list) — do not claim procurement is a first-class mobile workflow.
