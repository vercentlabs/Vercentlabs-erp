# Quality Pass 9 Research Summary

Pass 9 benchmarks enterprise quality-management patterns against official Microsoft Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management, Oracle Fusion Cloud Quality Management and Odoo 19 Quality documentation. The target treats Quality as an embedded control layer over Procurement, Stock, Manufacturing, Sales/Support and Assets rather than an isolated checklist database.

Key decisions are deterministic, versioned inspection truth; explicit sampling/measurement/tolerance semantics; active holds that block relevant material movement in a race-safe server transaction; governed disposition and CAPA; calibrated-instrument eligibility; lot/batch genealogy and CoA/document evidence; and cross-module effects only through public idempotent contracts. AI remains assistive and non-authoritative.
