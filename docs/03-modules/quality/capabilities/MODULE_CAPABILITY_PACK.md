## Cross-cutting controls
- immutable/versioned quality evidence and effectivity
- deterministic sampling/measurement/tolerance/pass-fail
- race-safe hard holds and maker-checker release/use-as-is
- public-contract disposition/rework/scrap/RTS and reconciliation
- calibrated-instrument validity and impact assessment
- lot/batch/serial genealogy, CoA and controlled documents
- tablet/mobile/barcode/WCAG 2.2 AA inspection workflows
- quality-cost/KPI formulas reconciled to source facts
