## Cross-cutting controls
- tenant/company/branch/site/asset scope and field-level financial/custodian controls
- effective dating, period locks, maker-checker and immutable financial/compliance history
- deterministic depreciation/value/disposal calculations and Accounting reconciliation
- barcode/QR, mobile/field evidence, offline boundaries and WCAG 2.2 AA
- idempotent public cross-module contracts, retries/reversals/reconciliation and observability
