|---|---|---|
| CAP-001 (enforce at confirmation, override, audit) | PASS | Credit is checked exactly once, at `confirmSalesOrder`, blocking with a stable `SALES_CREDIT_BLOCK` unless overridden; override requires `sales.credit.override` **and** a mandatory reason, and the resulting `credit_status` (`passed`/`blocked`/`overridden`) plus the override reason are both recorded in the `sales_order.confirmed` audit event — a real, auditable exception path, not a silent bypass. |

| **CAP-002 (unapplied payments, overdue debt) — real gap, recorded, not fixed this pass.** | FAIL | The exposure calculation is `SUM(base_currency_total)` across the customer's other `confirmed`/`on_hold`, not-fully-invoiced **Sales orders only** — it never looks at the customer's actual Accounts Receivable position in Accounting (`accounting_customer_invoices.outstanding_amount`, confirmed absent via grep) and never nets out advance payments (which, per F052, aren't even reconciled anywhere yet). This means credit exposure is blind to two of the dossier's explicitly-named factors: a customer who is genuinely overdue on invoices from *last quarter* with no new open Sales orders shows zero exposure and sails through the credit check; conversely a customer who prepaid a large advance still has that advance's value counted as if it were unsecured exposure. The check only ever sees "how much open Sales-order value exists right now," not the customer's real financial standing. |

## Net assessment (2026-09-06)

The concurrency and audit/override governance halves of credit-limit enforcement are excellent. The substantive gap is that "credit exposure" only means "other open Sales orders" — it has no visibility into the customer's actual Accounting AR balance (overdue invoices) or unapplied advance payments, both explicitly named in the dossier's own omission gate. A customer's real financial risk to the business is broader than what this check can see. Recorded for the gap-closing pass; meaningfully connects to F052's advance-payment reconciliation gap (fixing that would also give this check something real to net against).
