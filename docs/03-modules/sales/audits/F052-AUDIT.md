## Net assessment (2026-09-06)

Another instance of the "real validation logic wrapped around a table with no real downstream effect" pattern first found in F046 — this time on the financial side rather than the inventory side. Recording an advance payment doesn't actually create a financial transaction, and nothing ever reconciles a recorded advance into a final invoice. Recorded as a priority item for the gap-closing pass, in the same tier of severity as F046 given real money is involved.
