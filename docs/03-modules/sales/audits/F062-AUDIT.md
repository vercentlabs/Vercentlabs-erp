## Net assessment (2026-09-06)

A fitting last finding for the Sales trace: the delivery-to-invoice half of order-to-cash is genuinely well-built (verified repeatedly across F042/F047/F050/F051/F059), but the cash half — whether the customer actually paid — is completely unconnected. `sales_orders.payment_status` exists as a real column and is simply never written to by anything. Combined with F053 (credit checks blind to real AR) and F052 (advances never reconciled), this confirms a consistent theme across the whole back half of the Sales trace: Sales models its own internal states carefully, but the connective tissue to Accounting's actual financial reality is thin to nonexistent. Recorded for the gap-closing pass as the natural companion to F053's finding — likely the same underlying fix (a real Sales-to-Accounting payment-status sync) would close both.
