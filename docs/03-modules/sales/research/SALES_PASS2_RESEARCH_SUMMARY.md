# Sales Pass 2 Research Summary

Research date: 2026-08-30

The canonical F031–F062 register was preserved exactly. Official benchmark evidence was captured from Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales, SAP S/4HANA Sales, Salesforce Sales Cloud and Odoo Sales/Accounting. The synthesis requires quote→order→fulfilment→invoice/payment continuity, immutable/versioned commercial evidence, deterministic pricing/tax/credit/margin, partial/reversal workflows, public cross-module contracts and permission-safe reconciliation. Vendor-specific object/UI designs are not copied.
