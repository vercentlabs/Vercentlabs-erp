# Procurement Pass 3 Research Summary

Research date: 2026-08-30

The canonical F063–F096 register was preserved exactly. Official primary evidence was synthesized from Microsoft Dynamics 365 source-to-pay/procurement guidance, Oracle Fusion supplier registration, SAP S/4HANA supplier evaluation/invoice verification/scheduling agreements and Odoo 19 Purchase/Inventory/Accounting documentation. The target is enterprise source-to-pay behavior: governed supplier onboarding, requisitions/approvals, competitive sourcing, versioned POs/contracts, partial and quality-aware receiving, duplicate-safe supplier invoice intake, deterministic two-/three-way matching and landed cost, supplier performance/spend intelligence, and idempotent demand-driven replenishment/subcontract orchestration. Vendor-specific screen/object designs are not copied.

AI is assistance, extraction, drafting or recommendation only where specified. Deterministic supplier eligibility, approvals, awards, commitments, receipt quantities, matching tolerances, landed-cost allocation, inventory movement and accounting remain authoritative outside AI.
