# Procurement Journey — Requisition to Source and Purchase Order

Approved demand may proceed directly to an eligible supplier/PO or to competitive RFQ. RFQ invitations bind all suppliers to the correct event version; bids remain confidential until policy allows evaluation. Normalized comparison and award preserve evaluators, scoring, deviations and justification. Award creates at most one intended agreement/PO effect per idempotency key. PO approval/dispatch freezes the committed version; later material changes use amendment/reapproval and reconciliation.
