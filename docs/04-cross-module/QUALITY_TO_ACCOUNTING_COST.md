# Contract — Quality cost to Accounting

Quality classifies prevention/appraisal/internal-failure/external-failure events and provides source IDs, quantities, labor/material/return/scrap/claim context and dimensions. Accounting remains source of GL/account/currency/period truth. Quality cost reports reconcile to posted/approved Accounting and operational source evidence; closed-period corrections use Accounting reversal/adjustment rules, never direct Quality journal writes.
