# Contract — Quality disposition to Procurement return-to-supplier

Approved Quality RTS disposition provides supplier/receipt/item/UOM/lot/serial/eligible quantity, defect/NCR evidence, reason and stable disposition/idempotency ID. Procurement owns return authorization/document/vendor communication/credit matching; Stock owns physical movement/valuation; Accounting owns AP/credit posting. Quality stores resulting public IDs/status and reconciles failure/retry without direct private-table mutation.
