# Pricing and Margin Policy

## Principle

Vercentlabs ERP does not charge for each user. It charges for the operating capacity and service responsibility that create real cost: companies, branches, storage, API volume, automation, outbound communication, AI consumption, infrastructure, implementation and support.

## Margin guard

Provider fees vary by account, payment method, taxes and negotiated terms. The product therefore does not hard-code a claim about the payment provider's actual fee. Plan validation uses:

- a configurable payment-cost reserve, initially 4%;
- a per-plan estimated direct monthly cost;
- a configurable minimum software gross-margin floor, initially 70%.

A plan that fails the margin floor cannot be synced to Razorpay until the price, limits or estimated cost is corrected.

## Commercial rules

- No free-forever production plan.
- 14-day evaluation trial.
- Annual price is approximately ten monthly payments.
- Growth and Scale include mandatory paid implementation packages.
- Enterprise has a minimum platform commitment and separately scoped implementation.
- Taxes, communication providers, AI, data migration, custom integrations, dedicated infrastructure and exceptional support are not silently absorbed.
- Pricing must be reviewed after pilot usage, support load and actual infrastructure cost are measured.
