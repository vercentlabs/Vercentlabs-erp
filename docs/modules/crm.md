# CRM

Vercentlabs CRM owns enquiry capture, relationship history, follow-up execution,
qualification, opportunity pipelines, campaigns, sequences, forecasting,
account planning, buying committees, partner selling, field visits, governed
engagement, conversation intelligence, analytics, customization, privacy, data
quality and lead-to-customer conversion.

The core is provider-neutral. Email/calendar synchronization, conferencing,
telephony, messaging, transcription, enrichment and AI execution require
explicitly configured adapters and remain fail-closed when unavailable.

CRM hands converted commercial context to the Sales module. It does not own
quotations, CPQ, orders, contracts or invoices. It also does not own service
cases, SLAs, knowledge management or offline mobile synchronization.
