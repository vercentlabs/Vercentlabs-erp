# ERP-006 — CRM Lead Qualification

## Status

- Feature ID: F006
- Module: CRM
- Name: Lead Qualification
- Priority: P0
- Status: TESTING
- UAT: NOT_READY
- Dependencies: protected core foundation and F001–F005

F006 remains in testing until a named human completes the UAT matrix. Automated checks, browser automation, and live PostgreSQL evidence are technical verification, not human acceptance.

## Purpose, scope, and exclusions

F006 answers whether a Lead is sufficiently valid and commercially relevant to progress. It covers derived readiness, an intentional Qualified/Unqualified decision, reasons, requalification, immutable decision history, authorization, record/company/branch scope, audit, transactional outbox events, Lead Detail/list integration, responsive accessibility, tests, and UAT.

F007 stages/statuses, F008 duplicate detection, F009–F026 downstream sales/activity features, F027 scoring, F029 bulk actions, F030 reports, AI qualification, automation, and Lead-to-Opportunity conversion are excluded. F006 does not create an Account, Contact, Opportunity, Quotation, configurable questionnaire, score threshold, or qualification-rule administration feature.

## Pre-implementation audit

The repository originally represented qualification through the F001 lifecycle `crm_leads.status` values `qualified` and `unqualified`, with one free-text `unqualified_reason`. Create/Edit, list tabs, Kanban drag/drop, bulk update, and `POST /api/crm/leads/[id]/status` could all write those values. No first-class decision actor/time or qualification transition ledger existed.

The status route called `evaluateLeadReadiness`, which required company and product interest and, whenever any scoring rule existed, blocked a score below 20. That coupled F006 to F027. The Create drawer could begin a Lead as Qualified/Unqualified. Generic update, bulk, import, and offline paths could reach lifecycle status writes, producing inconsistent governance. Dashboard qualification totals counted `status='qualified'`. The score subsystem independently uses a historical grade named `qualified`; that grade is scoring context, not an F006 decision. Conversion was a separate explicit route and was not triggered by the old status route.

F001 already enforces a first name and at least one contact method for normal create/update. F004 source and F005 owner are independent fields. F005 record scope already limits records to the selected company/branch and, without `crm.records.view_all`, to unassigned or self-owned Leads. Existing audit and CRM outbox infrastructure is transaction-capable. No qualification-history model existed.

## Canonical architecture

`tenant.crm_leads.qualification_state` is the current decision source of truth. `crm_lead_qualification_events` is the append-only transition ledger. `lead-qualification.js` is the only domain service that evaluates readiness or changes the decision. `GET|POST /api/crm/leads/[id]/qualification` is the only web action API. The Lead Detail card and list filter consume those fields; lifecycle controls no longer qualify a Lead.

Generic create/update rejects canonical and legacy qualification fields. New Leads always use the database default `not_reviewed`. The old qualified/unqualified lifecycle values are migrated to the corresponding decision and normalized to lifecycle `working`; old rows without a trustworthy actor remain Not reviewed and still normalize to Working. This is the compatibility boundary until F007 owns lifecycle vocabulary.

## Qualification versus status, score, and conversion

Qualification is not lifecycle status. New, Contacted, and Working remain current pre-F007 lifecycle values; the decision independently uses Not reviewed, Qualified, or Unqualified.

Qualification is not scoring. Score and `lead_grade` may remain visible as historical F027-oriented context, but neither influences F006 readiness or decision validity. A score of -100 does not block a complete Lead; a high score cannot qualify an incomplete Lead.

Qualification is not conversion. F006 writes no Account, Contact, Opportunity, conversion record, or campaign conversion state and never calls the conversion service. The existing explicit conversion route remains separate and unchanged.

## State, readiness, reasons, and transitions

States are `not_reviewed`, `qualified`, and `unqualified`. Allowed real transitions are Not reviewed → Qualified, Not reviewed → Unqualified, Qualified → Unqualified, and Unqualified → Qualified. Same-state submissions are idempotent no-ops: no Lead write, history, audit, or outbox event.

Required readiness is derived from current data:

1. Lead identity: a nonblank first name.
2. Reachability: at least one nonblank email, mobile, or alternate phone.

Recommended, nonblocking context is company, job title, product interest, estimated value, and Lead source. Owner assignment is not required: commercial validity and ownership are independent. Readiness is never persisted and therefore cannot become stale.

Unqualification requires one governed code: No current requirement, Not a fit, Invalid or bad enquiry, Unable to reach, Budget unavailable, Duplicate or existing relationship, or Other. Other additionally requires at least three meaningful characters. A short optional decision note is stored with the current decision and event; it does not implement F017 general notes.

## Database

Tenant migration `062_crm_lead_qualification_f006.sql` adds the current state/reason/note/actor/time columns, state and decision-shape constraints, a qualification filter index, and `crm_lead_qualification_events` with organization/Lead/user foreign keys, checks, forced RLS, and an update/delete rejection trigger. Historical qualified/unqualified statuses are safely backfilled when an actor exists, one initial event is created without emitting integration events, and lifecycle status is normalized to Working.

Qualified decisions require actor/time and prohibit unqualification reasons. Unqualified decisions require actor/time and an allowed reason; Other requires details. Not reviewed prohibits decision metadata. The migration is transaction-safe, repeatable under the repository migrator, and populated-database compatible.

## Domain, APIs, authorization, and scope

`evaluateLeadQualificationReadiness` deterministically separates blockers and recommendations. `decideLeadQualification` validates authorization and input, locks the scoped Lead `FOR UPDATE`, rejects archived/converted Leads, checks readiness for qualification, writes state, inserts one history row, and inserts one outbox row in the same tenant transaction. `getLeadQualification` returns current decision, readiness, actor, reason choices, and bounded history.

- `GET /api/crm/leads/[id]/qualification` requires CRM view access and returns only a scoped Lead.
- `POST /api/crm/leads/[id]/qualification` requires same origin, CRM entitlement, billing write access, and `crm.leads.manage`.
- Organization owners act under established authority. Managers with `crm.records.view_all` may act across permitted records. Representatives may act only on Leads visible under F005 owner scope.
- Organization ID always comes from session context. Selected company/branch scope is applied server-side. Inaccessible and cross-organization identifiers return a safe not-found result.

Unassigned Leads are visible/qualifiable under the existing F005 policy. Reassignment changes who can subsequently view the decision/history but never changes the qualification itself. Source assignment/deactivation never changes qualification, and qualification never changes source.

## Bypass protection and integrations

Canonical and snake-case qualification fields plus legacy `unqualifiedReason` are rejected by generic Lead create/update. Create statuses Qualified/Unqualified are rejected. Generic status actions accept only New/Contacted/Working. Bulk qualification and legacy qualification statuses are rejected. Import and offline creation/update already delegate to canonical `createCrmRecord`/`updateCrmRecord`, so they cannot forge a decision. Public capture uses canonical Lead creation and begins Not reviewed. F029 bulk qualification and F021 import-controlled decisions remain unimplemented.

Each real transition inserts one immutable event with old/new state, reason, note, actor, and server timestamp. The API also writes one platform audit event and one PII-minimal transactional outbox event (`crm.leads.qualified`, `crm.leads.unqualified`, or `crm.leads.requalified`). Rejected and same-state requests write none. Row locking serializes simultaneous decisions; the committed order is reflected by the event sequence and the final row is valid.

## UX and accessibility

Lead Detail has one compact Commercial readiness card before profile context. It shows text state, required/recommended criteria, actor/time, current reason, actions, and progressively disclosed history. The native modal is 520px maximum on desktop, reflows on tablet, and becomes a full-height mobile surface at 430px and below. Actions have practical 44px targets; no readiness percentage or questionnaire is shown.

The list adds a compact text qualification badge and server-backed All/Not reviewed/Qualified/Unqualified filter without adding a dense table column. Native dialog semantics provide focus containment; the implementation supplies labelled title/description, Escape behavior, pending close protection, focus restoration, visible errors, text plus color state, live feedback, and reduced-motion rules.
