## Purpose, users, and exact scope

Contacts are individual people with whom the organization has a business relationship. CRM viewers can discover and read Contacts; users with `parties.manage` can create, edit, link/unlink Accounts, and archive them.

F003 includes the Contacts workspace, server-backed search, Account/status filters, pagination, standalone and Account-linked creation, Contact Detail, edit, soft archive, reachability validation, scoped Account selection, audit/outbox lifecycle events, responsive UX, accessibility states, tests, and UAT documentation.

F004–F030 remain excluded. F003 does not implement duplicate intelligence or merge UX, Opportunities, calls, meetings, tasks, follow-ups, notes/files, email history, timelines, territories, import/export, conversion, dashboards, scoring, tags, bulk actions, reports, enrichment, engagement, buying committees, or Contact AI.

## Pre-implementation repository audit

### Canonical persistence and relationships

- `tenant.contacts` is the single canonical Contact entity shared by CRM, Sales, master data, Lead conversion, Opportunities, communications, activities, account stakeholders, privacy compatibility features, quotations, and orders.
- Contacts reference F002 Accounts through `contacts.party_id → business_parties.id`; organization-aware FK migration 004 prevents cross-organization linkage.
- Before F003, `party_id` was mandatory. The schema had first/last name, designation, email, phone, mobile, primary flag, active/inactive status, privacy compatibility fields, audit users, timestamps, forced organization RLS, email/phone indexes, and no Contact code, owner, company, branch, department, or Contact-specific address.
- `tenant.addresses` belongs to `business_parties`, not Contacts. F003 displays linked Account location and does not create a competing Contact address model.
- Leads store `converted_contact_id`; Opportunities and communications store nullable `contact_id`; activities use `entity_type='contact'`. F003 archive never deletes or rewrites these relationships.

### Existing services and integration consumers

- Shared Business Data exposed `contacts` through `tenant.contacts`, required `partyId`, used `parties.manage`, and derived company scope through the related party. This compatibility contract remains available for cross-module/master-data consumers.
- Lead conversion already creates/reuses canonical `tenant.contacts` rows.
- Sales quotation/order option queries use active Account-linked Contacts. Standalone CRM Contacts intentionally do not appear in Account-specific Sales selectors until linked.
- Existing duplicate, merge, privacy, relationship-graph, communication, and account-intelligence services are outside F003. They remain compatibility internals and are not exposed in the canonical Contact UI.

### Existing web, API, mobile, navigation, and tests

- `/crm/contacts` loaded up to 500 Business Data Contacts and searched/filtered the bounded client payload through `BusinessDataManager`.
- `/crm/contacts/[id]` performed raw SQL in the page and emphasized Opportunities plus combined activity/communication/note timelines. It lacked canonical edit/archive lifecycle controls.
- No base `/api/crm/contacts` lifecycle contract existed; `/api/crm/[resource]` does not include Contacts because Contacts are shared master data rather than a generic CRM resource.
- Navigation already correctly placed Contacts under `CRM → Relationships` and required `crm.view`.
- Native mobile parity incorrectly claimed a native Contact detail although the native resource screen accepts only Leads, Opportunities, and Activities. F003 corrects Contacts to the secure responsive-web handoff.
- No focused F003 domain/API/web test or UAT document existed. ERP-003 and register rows were placeholders.

## Architecture decision

F003 extends `tenant.contacts` in place. It does not create `crm_contacts`, duplicate Account identity, or copy Account names. API/domain logic lives in `services/api/src/modules/crm/contact-operations.js`; Next.js routes remain auth/transport/audit boundaries.

Account linkage becomes optional. Linked Contacts inherit company visibility through their F002 Account. Standalone Contacts have no fabricated “Unknown Account” and are organization-wide because the approved Contact entity has no company/branch field. Contact ownership is not invented.

## Database changes

Migration `058_f003_crm_contacts.sql` makes `party_id` nullable, adds `archived_at`, adds forward-safe reachability and standalone-primary checks as `NOT VALID`, and adds lifecycle, Account-filter, and GIN identity-search indexes. Existing organization RLS remains enabled and forced.

`NOT VALID` preserves potentially incompatible historical production rows while enforcing the checks for new or changed rows. Local pre-migration inspection found three Contacts and zero without a reachable channel.

## Normalization, validation, and reachability

- First name is required, trimmed, and cannot be whitespace-only.
- Last name and job title trim; blank optional text becomes `null`.
- Email trims, lowercases, and must be syntactically valid.
- Email, mobile, or business phone is required; none is individually mandatory.
- Phone values preserve international prefixes, formatting, and extensions while rejecting obvious invalid text and unreasonable digit counts.
- Account ID must be a UUID, accessible in the current organization/company scope, and active for new/changed linkage.
- Archived Account relationships remain readable but cannot be newly assigned.
- PATCH validates persisted plus incoming state and only updates explicitly supplied columns.
- Changing Account clears a carried primary flag unless explicitly reassigned; unlinking always clears primary.
- Clients cannot set lifecycle status, company, branch, or owner fields directly.

The reachability invariant was selected because F003 is an operational CRM person record, every live Contact already satisfies it, and a Contact with no usable channel cannot support the feature's fundamental purpose. The forward-safe database constraint avoids destructive historical migration.

## API

- `GET /api/crm/contacts`: scoped search/filter/pagination
- `POST /api/crm/contacts`: standalone or Account-linked create
- `GET /api/crm/contacts/[id]`: scoped Contact detail and relationship counts
- `PATCH /api/crm/contacts/[id]`: partial update and Account link/unlink
- `DELETE /api/crm/contacts/[id]`: semantic soft archive

Errors reuse the CRM `{ ok, message, code, errors }` contract and include actionable codes such as `CRM_CONTACT_NAME_REQUIRED`, `CRM_CONTACT_CONTACT_METHOD_REQUIRED`, `CRM_CONTACT_EMAIL_INVALID`, `CRM_CONTACT_ACCOUNT_NOT_FOUND`, `CRM_CONTACT_SCOPE_FORBIDDEN`, and `CRM_CONTACT_NOT_FOUND`. SQL, table, constraint, and tenant-existence details are not returned.

## Search, filters, and pagination

SQL search covers first/last/full name, job title, email, mobile, business phone, and linked Account name. Status supports active/archived/all. Account filter uses the F002 Account ID after scope-safe lookup. The list defaults to 25 rows, caps API pages at 100, and never loads 500+ rows for client discovery.

## Authorization and scope

- Authentication and CRM entitlement are mandatory.
- `crm.view` governs Contact reads and navigation.
- `parties.manage` remains the canonical shared-Contact write permission.
- Writes additionally require billing write access, same-origin checks, and usage accounting.
- Forced RLS plus explicit organization predicates enforce tenant isolation.
- Linked Contact company visibility derives from `business_parties.company_id`; active company selection is respected even for elevated users.
- Standalone Contacts are organization-wide because no approved Contact company field exists.
- Branch and Contact owner/record scope do not apply; attempts to inject those fields are rejected.
- Scoped not-found behavior prevents direct-ID and Account-link IDOR disclosure.

## Account relationship and archive safety

The Account selector calls F002 server discovery with `status=active` and an eight-result limit. It is searchable, keyboard operable, mobile-safe, and does not preload Account data. Historical archived Accounts remain named and linked on Contact Detail.

Archive updates only Contact status, `archived_at`, and primary state. It does not delete the Account, Lead conversion, Opportunities, activities, communications, notes, attachments, quotations, orders, privacy records, or history.

## Audit and outbox

Create, update, and archive write `crm.contacts.created`, `crm.contacts.updated`, and `crm.contacts.archived` audit events within the same transaction client as the mutation. Matching `tenant.crm_outbox_events` rows are queued transactionally. Immutable audit snapshots contain IDs, Account relationship, status, primary state, and changed-field names rather than email/phone PII.

## Workspace and lifecycle UX

The canonical `/crm/contacts` workspace uses a concise heading, Create action, server search, status/Account filters, paginated desktop table, responsive cards, filtered/fresh empty states, retry state, and loading skeleton.

Create/edit use the established right-side focus-trapped CRM drawer with three sections: Contact identity, Company relationship, and Reachability. Save is disabled while pending, errors map to fields, dirty close asks for confirmation, and successful create navigates to Contact Detail.

`/crm/contacts/[id]` prioritizes identity, reachability, company context, historical Account state, governance timestamps, and small permission-safe Account/Opportunity/Activity links. It removes the old timeline-heavy primary experience without deleting legitimate compatibility data/services.

## Responsive design and accessibility

Desktop presents a six-column scan table and two-column detail sections. Tablet replaces the table with two-column cards and makes detail vertical. Mobile uses one-column cards/forms/filters/detail, full-width Create, sticky drawer actions, CRM mobile navigation, and no horizontal desktop tabs.

Semantic headings, labels, fieldsets, required/error associations, live alerts, combobox/listbox roles, arrow/Enter/Escape Account lookup, focus-visible treatment, focus-trapped drawer behavior, archive confirmation, status text, mail/tel links, 44px mobile targets, skeleton state, and reduced-motion handling are present.
