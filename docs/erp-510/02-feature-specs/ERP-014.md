## Business Purpose

F014 provides a governed CRM Meeting workflow for scheduling, logging, running, completing and cancelling meetings related to Leads, Opportunities, Accounts, Contacts, Campaigns or general CRM work. It keeps Meetings on the canonical `tenant.crm_activities` ledger so dashboards, search, timelines and Daily Work continue to see one activity system.

The feature also bridges the existing public meeting-booking/calendar subsystem into the same Meeting activity ledger. A customer booking therefore becomes visible to the host in CRM Activities without creating a parallel source of truth.

## Scope

F014 owns:

1. scheduled and completed Meeting creation;
2. Meeting subject, assignee, priority and related CRM record;
3. start/end date and time;
4. in-person, online, phone and other location modes;
5. scoped meeting join URL;
6. CRM Contact and guest-email attendees;
7. Meeting start, completion, cancellation and standardized outcome lifecycle;
8. actual start/end and bounded duration;
9. immutable Meeting lifecycle evidence;
10. optimistic concurrency and desired-state replay;
11. Web, mobile and offline Meeting workflows;
12. public booking → CRM Meeting activity bridging;
13. public booking reschedule/cancel synchronization;
14. PII-safe audit/outbox/history payloads.

F014 does **not** claim F015 Tasks, F016 Follow-ups and reminders, F017 Notes and attachments, F018 Email history, F019 Activity timeline, video-conferencing provider provisioning, calendar-provider OAuth/synchronization, recordings, transcripts, conversation intelligence or automated meeting summaries.

## Current Vercentlabs Code Audit

Before F014, the repository already had:

- `tenant.crm_activities` with `activity_type='meeting'` support;
- `tenant.crm_activity_attendees` for activity attendee persistence;
- `tenant.crm_meeting_links`, `tenant.crm_meeting_bookings`, calendar events and public booking routes;
- generic `/crm/activities` Web and mobile workflows;
- provider calendar/communications infrastructure.

The missing feature contract was a governed Meeting lifecycle. Generic Activities could mutate Meetings without Meeting-specific validation/evidence, public bookings were not represented in the canonical CRM activity queue, and Meetings had no immutable lifecycle history, standardized outcome, actual duration or dedicated mobile/offline contract.

## Domain / Service Logic

`services/api/src/modules/crm/meeting-operations.js` is the governed Meeting service.

It provides:

- `listCrmMeetings`;
- `getCrmMeeting`;
- `listCrmMeetingEvents`;
- `createCrmMeeting`;
- `updateCrmMeeting`;
- `startCrmMeeting`;
- `completeCrmMeeting`;
- `cancelCrmMeeting`.

### Validation and scope

- Organization scope is mandatory.
- Company/branch scope is inherited from the related CRM record when an all-company administrator creates a Meeting without an explicit selector.
- Related Lead, Opportunity, Account, Contact or Campaign is revalidated server-side.
- Assignee eligibility is revalidated server-side.
- Contact attendees use the actual Contact schema (`first_name`, `last_name`, `email`) and are limited to the Meeting's effective company.
- A Meeting can contain at most 100 attendees.
- A scheduled Meeting requires start/end times and end must be later than start.
- Meeting duration is limited to 24 hours.
- In-person Meetings require a location.
- Online Meetings require an HTTP(S) meeting URL.
- Completion outcome is one of `held` or `no_show`.
- Parent Lead/Opportunity activity timestamps change only after a real `held` completion.

### Concurrency and replay

- Real edits/start/complete/cancel use `expectedUpdatedAt` and `expectedStatus`.
- Exact desired-state retries return success without a second event/outbox mutation.
- Stale conflicting operations return actionable conflict errors.
- Booked Meetings cannot be independently edited/cancelled through the manual Meeting API; their booking workflow remains authoritative for reschedule/cancel.

## Existing Public Booking Integration

`bookMeeting` now serializes booking decisions on the owning Meeting Link row before checking for an exact previously committed booking. This prevents concurrent duplicate bookings and allows a lost-response retry to return the existing booking instead of falsely reporting the slot unavailable.

A successful public booking creates:

1. the existing Meeting Booking;
2. the existing Calendar Event;
3. the canonical `crm_activities` Meeting;
4. the existing activity attendee row;
5. immutable `crm_meeting_events` booked evidence;
6. a PII-safe outbox event.

Existing booking reschedule/cancel operations synchronize the linked Meeting activity and add Meeting lifecycle evidence.

## Generic / Offline Bypass Closure

Generic CRM Activity creation/update/archive/completion cannot create or mutate a Meeting lifecycle. Legacy server-side offline sync also rejects direct Meeting creation/completion. Web/mobile clients use the dedicated governed Meeting endpoints.

Lead Follow-up with `activityType='meeting'` is bridged into the governed F014 service so F016 behavior is preserved without claiming F016 itself.

## API

### Web

- `GET/POST /api/crm/meetings`
- `GET/PATCH /api/crm/meetings/:id`
- `POST /api/crm/meetings/:id/start`
- `POST /api/crm/meetings/:id/complete`
- `POST /api/crm/meetings/:id/cancel`

Mutations require same-origin validation, authenticated CRM Activities permission, billing write access, bounded usage accounting, tenant transaction and PII-safe audit evidence.

### Mobile

- `POST /api/mobile/v1/crm/meetings`
- `POST /api/mobile/v1/crm/meetings/:id/start`
- `POST /api/mobile/v1/crm/meetings/:id/complete`
- `POST /api/mobile/v1/crm/meetings/:id/cancel`

Mobile mutations use durable idempotency keys and the shared SDK. Offline queued Meeting actions replay through these governed endpoints rather than the generic Activity path.

## UI / UX

Meetings stay in the focused `/crm/activities?activityType=meeting` workspace.

The dedicated Meeting experience provides:

- schedule Meeting;
- log completed Meeting;
- edit scheduled Meeting;
- related CRM record selection;
- Contact attendee selection and additional guest emails;
- assignee, priority and location configuration;
- join link for online Meetings;
- start, complete and cancel actions;
- held/no-show completion outcome;
- immutable Meeting history;
- status/due/search filters and pagination;
- responsive controls with 44px minimum interaction targets and reduced-motion compatibility.

## Security and Privacy

- Server authorization is authoritative.
- Meeting reads/writes retain organization/company/branch/owner scope.
- Attendee Contact scope follows the Meeting's effective company even for all-company administrators.
- Attendee email addresses, meeting URLs and free-text notes/outcomes are not copied into immutable Meeting event payloads or Meeting outbox/audit snapshots.
- Sensitive values remain only on their scoped operational records where users need them.
